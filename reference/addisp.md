# addisp

addisp modified from pharmaverseadam

## Usage

``` r
addisp
```

## Format

A data frame with 1419 rows and 42 variables:

- STUDYID:

  Study Identifier

- USUBJID:

  Unique Subject Identifier

- SITEID:

  Study Site Identifier

- SUBJID:

  Subject Identifier for the Study

- PARAMCD:

  Parameter Code

- PARAM:

  Parameter

- AVALC:

  Analysis Value (C)

- DSSCAT:

  Analysis Subcategory

- ASTDT:

  Analysis Start Date

- ASTDTC:

  Analysis Start Date

- ASTDY:

  Study Day of Analysis Start Date

- AVALCSP:

  Specify text for AVALC

- S1EDT:

  Date of Last Exposure to Treatment

- S1EDTM:

  Datetime of Last Exposure to Treatment

- S1EDY:

  Study Day of End of Trt for Study Agt 1

- S2EDT:

  Date of Last Exposure to Treatment

- S2EDTM:

  Datetime of Last Exposure to Treatment

- S2EDY:

  Study Day of End of Trt for Study Agt 2

- S1SDT:

  Date of First Exposure to Treatment

- S1SDTM:

  Datetime of First Exposure to Treatment

- S1SDY:

  Study Day of Start of Trt for Stdy Agt 1

- S2SDT:

  Date of First Exposure to Treatment

- S2SDTM:

  Datetime of First Exposure to Treatment

- S2SDY:

  Study Day of Start of Trt for Stdy Agt 2

- PPROTFL:

  Per-Protocol Population Flag

- SAFFL:

  Safety Population Flag

- RANDFL:

  Randomized Flag

- SCRNFL:

  Screened Population Flag

- SCRFFL:

  Screen Failure Flag

- RESCRNFL:

  Re-screened Flag

- FASFL:

  Full Analysis Set Population Flag

- ENRLFL:

  Enrolled Population Flag

- AGE:

  Age

- AGEU:

  Age Units

- SEX:

  Sex

- RACE:

  Race

- COUNTRY:

  Country

- RFICDT:

  Date of Informed Consent

- TRT01P:

  Planned Treatment for Period 01

- TRT01PN:

  Planned Treatment for Period 01 (N)

- TRT01A:

  Actual Treatment for Period 01

- TRT01AN:

  Actual Treatment for Period 01 (N)
